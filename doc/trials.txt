<h2>Introduction
    This is a specification for a trial of the feasibility of using
    random variant vector graphic shape designs for Snakes
    for the game snakes and ladders.

<h2>Display Layout
    500 x 600px stage with white background, for drawing snakes (details below).
    Next button for re-drawing the snakes.

<h2>Snake Designs
    Eight random variant vector graphic snake shape designs should be displayed.

    The snakes should be from 90 to 432 pixels in length, including the forked
    tongue at the head. They should be sinusoidal within a range of 34px and a 
    maximum of 25px wide.

    The snakes should lie vertically with a maximum offset from the vertical
    at head or tail of 108px.

    The snakes should not overlap.

    The snakes should be bright colours, decorated with horizontal bands, spots,
    rings, diamond shapes or lengthwise squiggles.

<h2>Display Layout

    The stage should be 360 x 600px, with a yellowish off-white background and a grid 
    of 8 x 11 square(45 x 45px) grey bordered cells aligned with the top of the stage.

    Nine snakes using the methods for variants established at 
    Snake-Stage-5-Variants should appear on the board randomly such that:
    1) The tip of the tongue and the end of the tail sit in the centre of
        a grid square.
    2) The snake is aligned vertically, with a random variation such that
        the tail falls into a column one cell to the left or one cell
        to the right of the head.
    3) The snake is not less than three cells high.
    4) The snake is not more than nine cells high.
    5) The snake does not extend beyond the edges of the board (grid)
    6) No two snakes have heads or tails in the same cell
    7) Snakes do not touch or cross each other
    8) Snakes may appear in the same column, but not in contact with
        each other.

    There should be a Next Layout button below the stage.
